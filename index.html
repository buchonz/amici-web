About Amici

A taste of Italy in the heart of Georgetown

Amici — "friends" in Italian — is exactly how you will feel when you walk through our door on Church Street. Handmade pasta, pizzas from the oven, good wine and warm Guyanese hospitality: dinner with us is dinner with friends.
